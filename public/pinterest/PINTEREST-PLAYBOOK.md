## 2. Boards banayein (9)

Har board pe **description zaroor** likhein. Neeche copy-paste ready hai.

### Board 1 — Topsoil & Garden Bed Planning
**Description:** How much topsoil do you need? Calculate cubic yards, tonnes and bags for raised beds, borders and lawn topdressing. US and UK units.

### Board 2 — Gravel & Driveway Projects
**Description:** Work out gravel quantities for driveways and paths — depth, tonnes, cubic yards, and compaction allowance. Angular stone vs pea gravel.

### Board 3 — Concrete & Slab Calculators
**Description:** Concrete for slabs, footings and fence posts — ready-mix yards, bag counts, thickness and cost planning for patios and driveways.

### Board 4 — Paint & Wallpaper Calculators
**Description:** How much paint or wallpaper for a room? Coverage, coats, pattern match waste, and a planning cost band. Free calculators and guides.

### Board 5 — Flooring & Tile Planning
**Description:** Flooring boxes and tile counts with waste allowance for cuts, doorways, and pattern layouts. Measure once, order right.

### Board 6 — Roofing Estimates
**Description:** Turn building footprint and pitch into roof squares, bundles, or packs — with waste you can edit. Planning estimates, not a quote.

### Board 7 — Decking & Fence Projects
**Description:** Decking board counts and fence panel, post, and rail planning for straight runs on level ground.

### Board 8 — Home Renovation Budgeting
**Description:** Materials, labour, waste, and contingency rolled into a planning cost range. Compare quotes with numbers you can check.

### Board 9 — DIY Measuring Tips
**Description:** How to measure rooms, waste factors, US vs UK units, and the method behind every Project Home Calc tool.

## 3. Publish ka tareeqa (har pin)

1. Pinterest → **Create** → **Create Pin**
2. Image: `public/pinterest/<slug>-1000x1500.png` upload karein
3. `PIN-COPY.md` (ya CSV) se **Title / Description / Link / Board / Tags** copy karein
4. Tags **ek ek** search karke add karein (4–6 kaafi)
5. **Mark as AI-modified** = OFF
6. **Publish** (ya schedule — neeche schedule dekhein)

**Link hamesha www wala full URL** — homepage mat lagayein. Har pin usi page pe jaye jis ka image hai.

- Sham: 7–10pm

## Bulk import (recommended)

Do **not** use RSS for this site (no blog feed of pin images). Use the official CSV:

1. Deploy so images are live at `https://www.projecthomecalc.com/pinterest/*.png`
2. Open Settings → Import content → **Upload .csv or .txt file**
3. Upload `public/pinterest/PINTEREST-BULK-UPLOAD.csv`
4. Boards that do not exist are **created automatically**
5. Pins are scheduled ~4/day (Publish date column)
